Step-up verification for banking and finance

The number can be right.
The person can still be coached.

Attackers got into MGM Resorts and Caesars by phoning the help desk — every control worked, and the real user approved. EntraGuard calls the user, asks about their own sign-in seconds earlier, and listens to how they answer. If someone is feeding them the answers, it refuses.

Your Google sign-in is only used to start and own your verifications. Microphone audio is sent to Gemini for transcription and assessment and is not stored; transcripts are kept with your verification.

Run a verification
